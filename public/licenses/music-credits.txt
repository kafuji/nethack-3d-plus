NetHack 3D - Background music credits
=====================================

The in-game background music tracks below are dedicated to the public domain
under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Attribution is not required, but we gratefully credit the authors.
Files were trimmed, loudness-normalised and re-encoded to Ogg Vorbis.

The title theme (public/music/title-theme.mp3) is an original piece made for
NetHack 3D Plus: composed as a MIDI sketch (scripts/music-sketch) and produced
with Suno under a Pro plan subscription. It is not covered by CC0.
